# Code of Conduct

We want SAG Benchmark to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions relevant, constructive, and free from harassment.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private infrastructure details, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
